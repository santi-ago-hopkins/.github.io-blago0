# some things I learned so far in 2025

### how to live life
Life after school is definitely difficult. the general struggle that marked school all the way through undergrad really was enough to foster a sense of community. now after graduating its definitely harder to meet people... it's tough when people aren't your age, there's just not really that much shared experience. 

### derisk your regret 
there are so many decisions in life, some trivial and some so life-changing that you lie awake at night sorting through the pros and cons of each option. i realized that it's best to not think too long term, you should think in one year increments at most. one of the most important things to acknowledge is that what you want as a person will change, so it makes most sense to just chase whatever you want to chase now, and hope that your future self a year from now will do the same. 


### life is slow
it's hard to imagine that I need to live 60 some more years. some part of me wishes I didn't live that long. it feels that im obliged to take it slow since I'm expected to live long. im a naturally impatient person so its hard to wait


### stop asking why
It's such a human thing to always ask why. Always curious as to why something happened, why a person said this or that, or why am I like this? It is what it is. Instead of ruminating over every reasoning, often times it's just better to take it off the shelf.
