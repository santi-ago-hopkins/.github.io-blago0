### Jacob and the Angel
In bell hooks' ultimate chapter in her self-help book "all about love," she mentions the biblical story of Jacob wrestling an angel. In the story, Jacob physically jostles with an angel all night. When dusk hits and the angel must get on his way, the angel dislocates Jacob's hip bone. Still, while the angel is leaving, Jacob refuses to let the angel leave, urging the angel to bless him. 

Jacob is blessed, and will now carry a limp all the rest of his days. 

The pain is a blessing and the struggle will make you strong. 

"Consider it pure joy my friends whenever you encounter tribulations of any kind"
