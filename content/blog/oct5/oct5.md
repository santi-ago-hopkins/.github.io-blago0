### A Slow Sunday
Today was pretty slow. I promise it was intentional. The last 3 weeks have exhausted me, so just resting today felt nice. 

I had went to Wash Square at around 3p on a whim. Non-satirically, I was reading Marcus Aurelius' meditations. There was this girl on one of the other benches that seemed pretty cool. I'm not quite sure what it was exactly about her, but I wish I had talked to her then. I promised myself that if I see her again, I'll ask her to hang. Then again, she could easily be too old for me. Not sure how to approach this. 

### Culture Shock
Sometimes walking around San Francisco, and especially North Beach, I have this subtle tinge that I'm not supposed to be here. On any given weekend, you'd pass 50 ex-frat bros wearing a snapback that accents their 6" inseam shorts, white crew socks, and beat-ass club C's all before I see a single non-white or non-east asian person. I pray that I can build a friend group that isn't just white. I pray. 

This is all probably pretentious, given that I've exchanged zero words with this girl. Hope I see her again.
